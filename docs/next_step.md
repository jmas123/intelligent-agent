Make it truly life-aware, not just school-aware
The CV insight was accidental. Make it intentional. The system could maintain a lightweight life context model — are you in recruiting season? exam period? a light week? — and let that color every recommendation. "You have 3 assignments due but you also have 4 first-round interviews this week. Here's an honest picture of your week." No productivity app does this because none of them have the file + calendar + email awareness you've already built.

Add a memory layer that builds over time
Right now each interaction is essentially stateless in terms of learning. The most powerful upgrade would be a personal behavioral model that accumulates over weeks:

You always underestimate discussion posts (they take you 45 min, not 20)
You do your best work between 10pm-1am based on file activity timestamps
You tend to avoid starting anything the day it's assigned

After a full semester this becomes something genuinely irreplaceable — no one else has a model trained specifically on how you work. That's the moat.

Turn the "propose-only" model into a negotiation
Right now it proposes calendar blocks and you approve/reject. That's good but passive. Make it a back-and-forth:

"I'm proposing 8-10pm for the Debate brief. You have Zoom at 9 though — want me to move it to 10:30, or is the meeting optional?"

The system has enough context to have this conversation. That turns it from a scheduler into something closer to an actual assistant.

Build a semester debrief
At the end of each semester, generate a real retrospective:

Your average lead time before starting assignments (probably brutal and honest)
Which courses created the most last-minute crunch
Workload distribution across the semester — were you always front-loaded or back-loaded?
How accurate your time estimates were vs. reality

This is genuinely something universities can't give you. It's also the kind of artifact that would be striking to show in a portfolio or interview — proof that the system learned something real about you.
